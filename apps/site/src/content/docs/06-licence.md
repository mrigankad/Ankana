Ankana is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**. The full text is in the `LICENSE` file of the repository.

## What it means in practice

- **Using it for your own practice.** You can run, study and change Ankana freely. Nothing more is required.
- **Network use.** Anyone who uses a modified version of Ankana over a network must be able to get its complete source. If you run a modified Ankana for other people, publish your changes under the same licence.
- **Source link.** The app shows a Source code link to the public repository and the deployed commit, set through `ANKANA_SOURCE_URL` and `ANKANA_GIT_SHA`.
- **Swiss Ephemeris.** The ephemeris is used under its AGPL-3.0 licence from Astrodienst, which is why no separate professional licence is needed. Its data files fall under the same terms.
- **Your data.** Secrets, configuration, and practitioner and client data are not source and stay private.

Details are in `services/api-py/LICENSING.md`.

## Advisory

Ankana produces interpretive guidance for practitioners and their clients. Nothing it generates is medical, legal or financial advice, and reports carry an advisory line by default. Practitioners remain responsible for what they tell clients.
