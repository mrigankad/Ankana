## Who we are

[Practice name] ("we") is responsible for your personal data. We use the Ankana software, operated by
[Ankana entity], to store and process it on our behalf. Contact for privacy questions and requests:
[name, email, phone].

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Needed for |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | to contact you and identify your record | consultations, reminders |
| Date, time and place of birth | to calculate your birth chart | chart and report (**required** for the service) |
| Notes from our consultations | to remember what we discussed and advise you | consultations |
| Remedy plan and your check-ins | to track the remedies we suggest | remedy follow-up |
| (Optional) sending your report or messages by email or WhatsApp | to deliver your report | report sharing, only if you agree |
| (Optional) AI assistant help | an AI model may help us draft explanations from your chart; your name and contact details are not sent to it | only if you agree |
| (Optional) updates and offers from us | marketing | only if you agree |

You choose each purpose separately. You can say no to the optional ones and still receive our core service.

## How long we keep it

While you are our client, and then [N] years after your last interaction, after which we delete it
(we may keep records the law requires, such as invoices). You can ask us to delete it sooner.

## How we protect it

Your contact details, birth place and consultation notes are encrypted in our database. Access is limited to
people who need it. Data is stored in [India region]. We do not sell your data. The AI assistant, if you agree,
receives only your chart, not your name or contact details.

## Your rights

You can, at any time, ask us to: give you a copy of your data (we provide a downloadable file); correct it
(we keep a log of corrections); erase it (we will, unless a legal reason requires us to keep it, and we will tell
you); withdraw any consent (this will not affect what we did before, and you will receive a receipt);
nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to; and complain to us. If you are not satisfied you
may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Contact: [email/phone]. We respond within [30] days.
